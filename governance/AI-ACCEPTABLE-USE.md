# Responsible and acceptable use of AI systems (A.9.2, A.9.3)

## Objectives for responsible use
- AI supports human decisions about customers; it does not replace them where a decision
  has a significant effect (blocking an account, refusing a refund).
- Every AI output a customer sees can be explained or escalated to a person.

## Rules
1. The fraud score routes transactions to analysts. It must not be used for credit,
   marketing or profiling decisions.
2. The support assistant may only look up the authenticated customer's own records. Bulk
   reads are blocked above the session baseline and alerted to the SOC.
3. Staff must not paste customer personal data into external AI tools outside the
   approved, supplier-assessed integrations.
4. Any suspected misuse is reported through the concern channel in the AI Policy.
